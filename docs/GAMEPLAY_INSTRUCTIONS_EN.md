# Duplo TD — How to Play

## What It Is

`Duplo TD` is a tower defense experience for Hytale.

Your goal is to defend the `Hollow`, build towers and traps, improve your build with modules, and survive increasingly dangerous enemy waves.

## Where To Start

If this is your first time playing, start with the `Tutorial`.

The tutorial explains:

- tower placement
- wave starting
- trap usage
- module rewards
- super towers
- shard spending after a match

## Main Goal

Do not let enemies reach the `Hollow`.

If the Hollow runs out of health, the match is lost.

## How A Match Works

A normal match usually looks like this:

1. Go to the `Operator`.
2. Choose a difficulty.
3. Place at least `1` tower or trap.
4. Start the wave.
5. Defeat enemies, earn gold, and strengthen your defense.
6. Between waves, build more towers, upgrade your setup, and adapt your strategy.

## Towers, Traps, And Super Towers

There are three main types of defense in the game:

- regular towers
- traps
- super towers

Regular towers are the core of your defense.

Traps help control the route and trigger at important moments.

Super towers provide strong unique effects and can change the course of a match.

## Modules

During a run, you will receive module choices.

Modules strengthen your build and help you adapt to the current situation.

If you accidentally close the module selection window, you can reopen it through the `Operator`.

## Shards And Permanent Progression

After matches, you earn `Shards`.

Shards can be spent at the `Upgrade Keeper` for permanent progression between runs.

If you lose a match, spend your shards at the Keeper and start a new run.

## SOLO

In `SOLO`, you play alone and manage the full defense by yourself.

This is the best mode for learning the map and understanding the core mechanics.

## DUO

In `DUO`, you play with a second player on a dedicated map.

Before the match starts:

- choose your side at the `Side Selection` NPC
- travel to `DUO` through the teleport
- begin the match at the `Operator`

In `DUO`, coordination matters. Watch your side, work with your ally, and support the shared defense.

## Recommended Order For New Players

- complete the tutorial first
- play a few `SOLO` matches
- move on to `DUO`
- spend shards after defeats to improve your long-term progression

## In Short

- defend the `Hollow`
- build towers and traps
- start waves at the `Operator`
- choose modules
- use super towers
- earn shards and improve your progression
